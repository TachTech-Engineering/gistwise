# Cold-reader brief

Use this to test whether an explanation works for someone who did not write it. Send it to a fresh subagent. Give the subagent only what is below the line: no source material, no conversation history, and no hint about what the text is supposed to say. The test is only worth running if the reader starts as cold as the real one.

---

You are the intended reader of the text below. Read it once, the way that reader would, then answer from what the text gave you. Do not fill gaps from your own knowledge. If you already know the topic, notice where you were relying on that knowledge instead of the text, and report it as a gap.

**You are:** [the reader profile, e.g. "a smart person with no background in security" or "an engineer new to this codebase"]

**Text:**

[the final text]

## Answer

1. **Gist.** In one sentence, the one thing this text wants you to remember.
2. **Mechanism.** In two or three sentences of your own words, how the thing works. Don't reuse the text's phrasing.
3. **First stumble.** Quote the first sentence where you had to reread or got lost, and say why. Write "none" if there was none.
4. **Unearned terms.** Any technical term the text used before explaining it.
5. **Next step.** If the text asked you to decide or do something, what is it? Write "none" if it did not.

Keep the whole answer under 150 words.
